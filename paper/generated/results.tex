The nominal eight-robot priority-minus-equal difference in completion probability is 4.0000 percentage points (95\% paired interval [-6.0000, 14.0000]; 50 pairs).

The nominal eight-robot priority-minus-equal difference in mean task latency is -0.0036 s (95\% paired interval [-0.0045, -0.0028]; 50 pairs).

The nominal eight-robot priority-minus-equal difference in mean episode FoV inconsistency is -1.6778 percentage points (95\% paired interval [-5.9000, 0.7667]; 50 pairs).

These results quantify the complete closed loop rather than only the static optimizer. Queueing, changing CPU service, delayed self and peer state, discrete route changes and the frozen policy can change mission outcomes independently of snapshot objective gains. The confidence intervals and sensitivity figures must therefore be considered alongside the mean values; superiority is not assumed.
